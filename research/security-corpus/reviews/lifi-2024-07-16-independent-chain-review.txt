Independent LI.FI chain-evidence review

Disposition: the narrow development-only USDT effects oracle is defensible,
subject to a separate primary-source label and admission decision. This is not
full Ethereum support, a security verdict or score, a victim pre-sign result,
or a blind holdout. I made no new RPC calls and changed no existing manifests
or evidence packets.

I inspected the saved original request, signed type-2 transaction, receipt,
complete block transaction array, trace, prestate, state diff, and every
original-position balance/allowance request and response. I reran the
preparer's 71 offline assertions and independently executed 91 checks recorded
in the companion JSON. I did not rely on validation.json alone. The independent
checks recompute the signed transaction hash, the 20-field Cancun block-header
hash, eight runtime fingerprints, token mapping keys, and the diamond's
dispatcher storage key. These establish internal consistency of the captured
evidence; they do not verify trie inclusion, consensus, or signer recovery.

the result. Independently deriving the key for selector 0x3d86ea87 gives:
0xa5b9829b98c8de228bae21a0c67c7edd99079f381fed21392089a89b91e158f5
Its value and the actual DELEGATECALL both identify facet:
0xf28a352377663ca134bd27b582b1a9a4dad7e534

All six existing runtimes and both temporary CREATE outputs match their saved
SHA-256 and Keccak-256 fingerprints. This is execution provenance. No source
compilation or immutable source revision was independently verified.

Two temporary contracts are created, called with one wei each, and then
self-destruct to the diamond within the same transaction. The diamond passes
two wei onward. Both temporary accounts are absent from the final pre/post
diff. Unchanged final account sets or absent post.code alone therefore cannot
establish that no intermediate creation/deletion occurred. Preserve the full
trace and its lifecycle limitations in any fixture or coverage result.

Native-fee limitation and provider dependence

Five read diffs report nonce-only effects; the pre-position allowance query
reports the exact effective gas fee and beneficiary tip. The original
transaction's fee and tip independently reconcile, but the query wrappers
are inconsistent. Direct token reads and original token storage corroborate
the narrow USDT oracle. They do not establish arbitrary-contract native-balance,
gas-context, or fee fidelity. Do not weaken production historical-context
validation merely to force this case through the current engine.

Only dRPC supplies successful execution/state/position evidence. PublicNode
supplies transaction metadata and chain ID but returns -32601 for the requested
transaction tracers. There are no Merkle proofs or independent execution-client
results. The companion JSON fingerprints 33 retained JSON artifacts, including
the original transaction metadata packet. Stored responseBodySha256 fields
cannot independently reconstruct original JSON whitespace from parsed data.

A second archive execution source and reproducible compilation would improve
corroboration, but are not admission requirements I would impose on this
explicitly limited single-provider development effects regression. Primary
incident attribution is outside this chain review and requires the separate
source review and admission decision.

Use boundary

Use only the recorded USDT effects, separate account roles, unchanged maximum
allowance, and lifecycle/selector interpretation requirements. Keep incident
labels, postmortems, effects-oracle contents and incident-bearing file paths
out of detector inputs. The full incident family belongs to development after
exposure. The missing victim approval intent/signing screen, matched legitimate
controls and untouched incidents prevent claims of victim pre-sign performance
or blind security evaluation. This evidence does not add chain-1 coverage to
the current chain-999 engine.
